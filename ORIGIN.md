# AWARE Origin

**AWARE shares its name with a 2013 logistics system. The 2013 version is archived separately.**

---

## 2013: Autonomous Warehouse Automated Resource Engine

AWARE was first conceived in 2013 as the **Autonomous Warehouse Automated Resource Engine** — a logistics and supply chain optimisation system using a Queen-Worker architecture. It automated resource allocation in warehouse environments, coordinating multiple worker agents under a central queen agent to optimise inventory movement and storage.

---

## 2026: Agent Governance Framework

In 2026, the AWARE team pivoted the project to an **agent governance framework** — applying the same core principles of multi-agent coordination and resource allocation to the challenge of orchestrating AI agents in production environments.

The framework now provides:
- **Agent Registry & Identity** — trust domains, authentication, session binding
- **Pheromone Routing** — task-specific routing with quality-gated updates
- **Security Control Plane** — anomaly detection, tool access control, compliance mapping
- **GitOps Agent-as-Code** — decision chain traceability, configuration versioning

---

## Why the Same Name?

The 2026 framework deliberately retains the AWARE name:

- **Brand recognition** — the original AWARE project had established credibility in logistics and automation circles
- **Team continuity** — the same research group that built the 2013 system is behind the 2026 framework
- **Conceptual lineage** — both systems solve resource allocation and coordination problems; the 2026 version generalises the approach

---

## Relationship

The 2013 AWARE and 2026 AWARE are **separate systems** with different codebases. They share a name, team, and conceptual heritage — but not a codebase.

The 2013 AWARE mindmap is preserved at `docs/heritage/AWARE-2013.mm`.
